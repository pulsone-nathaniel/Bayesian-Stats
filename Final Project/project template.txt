Goal: 
	General: study the relationship between biometric data and patient history and one's risk for diabetes
	Data: present data table, source, values
	Scope: Create a Bayesian logistic regression model to classify and predict whether a woman has diabetes based on their known data
	Method:
		Split data into training and testing
		Use variable selection to find a selection of candidate models
		Use Stan to estimate posterior for subset models, analyze diagnostics, compare contrast
		Judge predictive performance in classification 
	Interpretation
	Analysis, conclusions, weaknesses, etc.
